Welcome — this page is yours.

Your assistant just published it. It's live on the internet right now, at its own link — and that link is the whole idea.

A patch page is a simple web page your assistant makes for you: a plan, a report, a proposal, anything worth showing someone. Instead of attaching a file or pasting a wall of text, you send a link that looks good on any screen.

  • Shareable

    Send the link to anyone

    Anyone who has the link can view the page — no account, no app. It isn't listed or searchable, so only the people you send it to will find it.

  • Updatable

    The link stays, the page evolves

    Ask your assistant to change a page and it updates in place. Everyone you already sent the link to simply sees the newest version.

  • Long-lived

    Stays up while it's being read

    Pages stay online as long as people are visiting them. A page nobody has opened in a few months is quietly retired to keep the service free.

  • Deletable

    Gone when you say so

    Ask your assistant to take a page down and it's removed for good. Only this computer can change or delete your pages — nobody else.

Try it: next time you have a plan or a write-up, tell your assistant make this a patch page — and you'll get a link like this one back.
